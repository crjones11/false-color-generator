False Color Generator

☕ Buy me a coffee

Clip-anchored false color LUTs from a camera profile. Warm colors count down to clip, cool colors count up from the noise floor.

Camera

    Each ISO gets a Full and a Legal LUT. Full is for in-camera slots and devices that scale their own output; Legal has the output colors scaled to 64-940. A report and an OmniScope preset (untested) are included.
